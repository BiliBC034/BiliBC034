# 🔭 Featured Work:
**🛰️ Opportunistic positioning with LEO satellites** · SPCOMNAV (IEEC) & bachelor's thesis<br>
Experimental testbed that captures real Starlink signals with an SDR and extracts Doppler observables in MATLAB, exploiting their similarity to 5G signals, to complement GNSS in harsh environments.

**🏕️ Revenue Intelligence platform for campsites** · Talenbox & bachelor's thesis<br>
Rate shopper that collects competitor prices and reviews, combined with AI occupancy forecasting served through a web dashboard for Premium users, running on AWS and Firebase.
